# VALLERIAN

### Personal Brand Identity

## 01. Brand Overview

Brand Name: VALLERIAN

Founder: Vallerian Dava Dayoh

Location: Manado, Indonesia

Professional Title: Independent Visual Storyteller

Primary Tagline: An Ongoing Study of Seeing.

Brand Description:

An independent visual storyteller exploring the relationship between people, places, and moments.

VALLERIAN is a personal creative space that documents visual exploration, creative experiences, and personal perspectives through photography, cinematography, and other visual mediums.

## 02. Brand Purpose

VALLERIAN memiliki empat tujuan utama:

* Personal Archive. Mendokumentasikan perjalanan, pengalaman, dan perkembangan kreatif.
* Professional Portfolio. Menampilkan kemampuan dan karya untuk membuka peluang profesional.
* Personal Branding. Membangun identitas sebagai independent visual storyteller.
* Creative Expression. Menyampaikan perspektif, cerita, dan ekspresi melalui berbagai medium visual.

VALLERIAN harus berkembang bersama perjalanan kreatif pemiliknya.

## 04. Creative Philosophy

### A Study of Seeing

Setiap orang memiliki perspektif berbeda ketika melihat dunia.

Dua orang bisa berada di tempat yang sama dan menyaksikan momen yang sama, tetapi menghasilkan cerita yang berbeda.

VALLERIAN menjadikan setiap karya sebagai representasi dari cara pemiliknya mengamati, merasakan, dan memahami sesuatu.

Photography is a way to notice and preserve something that might never happen again.

Cinematography is a way to translate feelings and stories through visual sequences.

Core Statements:

"I create because I find meaning in the little things people often overlook, and I want to give those moments a place to exist."

"Through my lens, I want to show the world through my perspective, capturing the connection between people, places, and moments."
